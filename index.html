TOP / PROFILE

I'm André (fool_in_love), in Four Corners, Florida. ドヴォルザーク is for Vernon Dvorak, who worked out how to read a hurricane's strength from satellite pictures.

I make mallsoft and vaporwave as MIDNIGHT ATRIUM in Bitwig Studio, and I track tropical and severe weather around Central Florida: NWS Melbourne forecast discussions, pressure in hPa, NEXRAD in GR2Analyst and RadarScope.

Languages: English, Português, learning Français.
Off-screen: alto saxophone, tennis.
On-screen: osu!, Roblox, Forza Horizon 6.
Daily drivers: CachyOS + GNOME, Fish, Firefox with uBlock Origin.
